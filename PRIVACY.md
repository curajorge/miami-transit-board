# Privacy

Miami Transit does not require an account and does not collect, sell, or share personal information.

The Android app requests only Internet access. Current trip selections remain in memory and reset when the app reloads. This version does not read older prototype selections that may remain in local app storage. The app does not request precise location permission, contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone, SMS, or notification access.

Map tiles are requested directly from OpenStreetMap infrastructure, which can infer the approximate map area being viewed from requested tile coordinates. Trolley data is read from the public City of Miami tracker, and bus arrivals are read from Miami-Dade BusTime. These services receive ordinary network information such as an IP address when requests are made.

This independent project is not affiliated with or endorsed by the City of Miami or Miami-Dade County.
